Is it really free?

Yes during the beta: 100 messages a day, up to 25 in any 5-hour window. A paid Pro plan with higher limits is planned.

Where are my chats stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing browser data erases them, so use Settings → Usage & data to export a backup.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. Like any AI it can make mistakes. It cites its sources so you can check, but verify anything important.

What can't it do yet?

No accounts or sync between devices, and no voice conversations. Image creation depends on availability and may occasionally be unavailable.
